# Scruple Ultimate Strategy

## The end game
Providers (fal.ai, Stability AI, Replicate, Adobe, Midjourney, etc.) license Scruple and embed it natively in their own generation services. Scruple becomes infrastructure, not a competing product.

**Why:** Providers have the distribution. Scruple has the technique (patent-pending API-level interception for provenance). B2B licensing = far higher margins and leverage than consumer product.

## What scruple.ai actually is
A **reference implementation** — proves the technique works, generates real user data, builds the provenance credibility needed to approach providers. Also generates some direct revenue from lock actions.

## Launch providers (chosen 2026-04-09)
1. **fal.ai** — prompt-based + ComfyUI workflows natively, H100/H200/B200, needs enterprise terms for output ownership clarity
2. **ComfyDeploy** — user brings their own ComfyDeploy account + API key, pays ComfyDeploy directly, Scruple witnesses only. Scruple never touches GPU cost.

ComfyDeploy model: user pays ComfyDeploy for GPU, pays Scruple for provenance actions (locks). Clean separation.

## The licensing pitch to providers
"Your users are already generating. Add one line to your pipeline — Scruple witnesses every generation. Users get provenance certificates. You get a differentiated product. We get a license fee."

The patent on API-level interception is the moat. Providers can't build around it without licensing.

## What NOT to do
- Don't become a GPU/compute provider
- Don't compete with fal.ai, Replicate, Stability AI on generation
- Don't build your own ComfyUI cloud (ComfyDeploy solves that)
- Don't add providers that require enterprise negotiation before the patent is filed

## Filing priority
Patent must be filed before approaching any provider for licensing. The technique needs protection before it's disclosed in a sales conversation.
